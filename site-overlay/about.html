BACKGROUND

Composition, research, and practice.

Education, professional experience, awards, and selected intellectual property.

Music is the starting point.

I compose music and build creative systems that keep human intention at the centre. My work connects composition, symbolic music analysis, and human–AI interaction, with an emphasis on making musical decisions visible, editable, and reviewable.

I founded Xunpu Studio / 讯谱 Studio, an integrated music-learning, analysis, creation and practice workbench. My computational music work Algorithmic Awakening received the Best AI Music Production Award at the 2nd HKUST AI Film Festival × MiniMax in 2026.

My composition, computing and music-industry training meet in creator-facing tools and original musical works. I am interested in how these tools fit professional workflows, preserve authorship and help musicians make decisions they can inspect and revise.
