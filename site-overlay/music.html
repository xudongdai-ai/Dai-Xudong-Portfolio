Algorithmic Awakening

🏆 Best AI Music Production Award · 2nd HKUST AI Film Festival × MiniMax · 2026

A computational music work that treats sound as an invisible film: discrete pulses develop into mechanical motion and expanded, organic textures.

I used custom code to generate serial and random-sequence materials, then selected and transformed them through composition, arrangement, sound design, mixing and mastering in Studio One. Form, pacing and musical selection remained human-led.
