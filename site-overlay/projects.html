Integrated music learning, analysis, creation & practice workbench

An integrated music-learning, analysis, creation and practice workbench for music students, composers and emerging creators. Reviewable analyses of harmony, counterpoint and form connect with AI-assisted composition, twelve-tone tools, orchestration support, ear training, rhythm feedback and an AI music advisor.

MusicXML/MXL/MIDI and PDF/image input via OMR are entry routes into the workbench. Musical rules, score annotations and explanations allow users to inspect, question and revise the system's reasoning. I lead creator research, product design, prototyping and development coordination.

Twelve-Tone Magic Cube

Interactive generation of twelve-tone matrices through transposition, inversion, and retrograde. Musical selection and mapping turn a transparent pitch grammar into compositional material.

Desktop Python and mobile JavaScript implementations produced identical matrices for 20 tested rows. I choose fragments, register, density, rhythm, and timbre to shape the result.

Product & application development2026–present

ReelMesh

A human-in-the-loop generative AI production system connecting projects, editable workflows, assets, generation tasks, and creator review. Creator control, approval, and selective regeneration keep creative decisions visible and revisable.

My contribution covers product workflow, interface implementation, and first-party application development. Built with React, Node.js, SQLite, and Electron; shared dependencies retain their respective licences.

Human-in-the-Loop InteractionMulti-modal Storytelling Workflow

ReelMesh provides a creator-facing workspace for examining human decisions across planning, production, review and editing. It offers a basis for future user-centric workflow studies; a formal user evaluation is not reported here.
