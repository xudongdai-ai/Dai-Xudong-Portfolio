MUSIC STRUCTURE / ONGOING RESEARCH

Musicology-Guided Structural Attention (MGSA)

Measure-Level Boundary Detection in Symbolic Music

Independent research · Manuscript in preparation · Preliminary validation
RESEARCH QUESTION

Can explicit musicological priors improve measure-level boundary detection in symbolic scores?

MUSICOLOGICAL PRIORS

Metric + Motif + Harmony + Cadence + Repetition − Boundary

A = softmax(QKᵀ / √d + ∑ λᵣ Bᵣ − λᵦ Bᵦ)V

The sum covers Metric, Motif, Harmony, Cadence and Repetition. Nonnegative weights control each bias; Boundary enters as a separate subtractive term.

MusicXML→Measure Encoder→MGSA→Measure-Level Boundary Prediction

Current prototype · Boundary / phrase-ending pilot on Mozart symbolic scores, with baseline and prior-ablation experiments.

Research targets · Independently supervised section relations and global form decoding remain to be completed.

Data & annotation pipeline. The pilot uses sonata-grouped splits from the DCML Mozart piano-sonata corpus, with phrase-boundary projections and cadence supervision from its annotations. Score-derived input features are separated from annotation supervision. Preprocessing records grouped splits, annotation audits, and data fingerprints. These are derived research assets, not a newly authored corpus.

Reading the comparison. MGSA integrates all 6 musicological logit biases on top of the learned pooling backbone. Mean pooling → learned pooling changes the pooling architecture; learned pooling without priors → MGSA isolates the added prior biases under matched pooling. These pilot differences do not establish consistent or statistically significant gains.

Xunpu GraphOMR: Explainable Optical Music Recognition

Research prototype · Typed symbol graphs + rule-based decoding

Score images become typed symbol graphs; geometric relations and musical consistency checks support inspectable reconstruction into MusicXML, MIDI, and diagnostics.

The validated baseline covers printed single-staff melodies in treble clef, C major and 4/4. Current evaluation uses 16 variants of two synthetic fixtures.
